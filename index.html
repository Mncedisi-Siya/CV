I'm a civil engineer with a BSc (Hons) in Civil Engineering, specialising in water resource engineering and construction management.

At EWADE I work as Employer's Agent and Resident Engineer on earth dams, potable water, irrigation and resettlement housing, under FIDIC, GCC and JBCC contracts. That covers community engagement and feasibility, surveying and design, BOQs and tenders, cost control, payment certificates, variations and claims, site quality, testing and commissioning.

Most of my projects sit in rural communities, so I work as closely with chiefdoms and farmers as with contractors, consultants, funders and government departments.
